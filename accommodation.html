Highland Thrills

Things to Do in Lesotho

Whether you crave high-altitude adrenaline or peaceful cultural immersion, the Kingdom has adventures for every style of explorer.

  • Basotho Pony Trekking: Traverse highland trails atop sure-footed native ponies
  • Alpine Skiing & Snowboarding: Carve real snow at 3,222m at AfriSki Resort
  • Guinness World Record Abseil: 204m abseil down Maletsunyane Falls
  • Scenic 4x4 Mountain Driving: Scale the hairpins of Sani Pass and Baboons Pass
  • Reservoir Boat Cruises: Explore the alpine fjords of Katse Dam
  • San Rock Art Tours: Discover ancient hunter-gatherer sacred paintings

Seasonal Guide

Best Time to Visit

Lesotho is a vibrant year-round destination — each season brings its own distinct mountain magic.

🌸
Spring (Sep–Nov)

Wildflowers carpet the highland valleys. Moderate temperatures perfect for pony trekking and ridge hikes.

☀️
Summer (Dec–Feb)

Lush green landscapes with roaring waterfalls at peak flow. Pleasant days with dramatic afternoon thunderstorm skies.

🍂
Autumn (Mar–May)

Golden alpine grasses and crystal-clear azure skies. The absolute best season for landscape photography.

❄️
Winter (Jun–Aug)

Snow dusting high peaks! Skiing and snowboarding at AfriSki. Freezing night skies offering dazzling stargazing.
