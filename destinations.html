Tourist Destinations

Explore spectacular gorges, arch dams, alpine ski peaks, and sacred royal historical monuments across Lesotho.

Maletsunyane Falls

One of Africa's highest single-drop waterfalls (192 m). Famous for wild canyon horse treks and the world's highest commercial abseil.

Katse Dam

Africa's highest arch dam (185 m wall), nestled between mountain fjords. Experience dam interior tours, botanical gardens, and boat cruises.

Lesotho's premier UNESCO World Heritage Site, famed for surreal sandstone rock arches, pristine rock pools, and ancient San rock art.
