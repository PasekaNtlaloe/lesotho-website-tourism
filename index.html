Why Lesotho?

Why Visit the Kingdom in the Sky?

  • Completely encircled by South Africa, yet with its own distinct currency and kingdom
  • 100% of territory sits above 1,000 m — the world's highest minimum elevation
  • Skiing in Africa! AfriSki offers ski lifts, snow parks, and mountain dining
  • Maletsunyane Falls holds the Guinness Record for the world's highest commercial abseil
  • Ride the sure-footed Basotho pony through mountain passes and traditional villages
